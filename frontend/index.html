    Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) Formulation

    RRF solves the problem of combining score distributions with different dimensions and scales (cosine similarity in [-1, 1] vs unbounded BM25 scores):

    RRF_Score(d) = (w_dense / (k + rank_dense(d))) + (w_sparse / (k + rank_sparse(d))) Constants in OmniRAG: - k = 60 (rank smoothing constant, prevents top items from dominating) - w_dense = 0.65 (semantic importance) - w_sparse = 0.35 (exact keyword importance)

    Corrective RAG (CRAG) State Machine

    [Candidate Chunks] ---> [Evidence Evaluation Node] | +-----------------------+-----------------------+ | (>0.65 Confidence) | (0.38 - 0.65) | (<0.38 Confidence) v v v [CORRECT State] [AMBIGUOUS State] [INCORRECT State] Synthesize Answer Reformulate Query & Activate Guardrail: with exact badges trigger secondary pass Refuse to hallucinate

    The RAG Triad (Production Evaluation)

    To avoid subjective impressions in production and interviews, OmniRAG calculates:

    • Context Precision: What fraction of retrieved context chunks are directly relevant to the user query?
    • Faithfulness (Groundedness): Are the factual statements generated by the LLM strictly grounded in the context without fabricated numbers?
    • Answer Relevance: Does the answer address the question asked rather than giving a generic reply?
